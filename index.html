Technology, Identity, and Society

Welcome! This website explores how technology intersects with identity, diversity, and implicit bias. Through research, reflection, and real-world examples, we examine how technology shapes our everyday experiences and impacts different communities.

Why This Topic Matters

Technology affects nearly every aspect of modern life. Understanding bias, representation, and inclusion helps us become more thoughtful users, designers, and future professionals in an increasingly digital world.
